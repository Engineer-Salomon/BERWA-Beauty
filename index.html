Common Queries

Booking & Studio Inquiries

Do you accommodate off-site / hotel bookings?

Yes, our master stylists are available for hotel suite or private residence calls for special red-carpet occurrences and weddings. Additional flat travel concierge fees apply depending on distance.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

We require at least 24 hours' notice for rescheduling or cancellations to avoid a 50% reservation charge. Cancellations within 4 hours of the appointment time incur a full charge.

Which clean beauty brands do you use in-studio?

We strictly utilize premium, clean hair dyes and clean skin products certified toxic-free. This includes organic botanical color systems, and vegan, high-pigment cosmetics that preserve your natural glow.
